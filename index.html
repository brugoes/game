🐕 Hero Dog Walk 🐾

Bruna, Mario, Max and Mittens are off on a one-mile adventure!

Grab the leash, we're off today!Max and Mittens lead the way!Frogs and sprinklers, watch them go!Hero walkers, go go go!

The goal

Walk the 1-mile loop from Bruna's house and get home faster than anyone else. The timer runs the whole way, and the fastest time wins.

How to play

  1. Pick Bruna or Mario as your hero.
  2. Pick Max or Mittens to take for a walk.
  3. Your dog walks on its own. Hold Space (or the Hurry button) to go faster.
  4. Fix every problem fast, then keep walking.

Trouble on the street

  • 🐸 Frogs and 🐕 mean dogs distract your dog and make it stop. Give a treat (your dog sits and refocuses), use the water spray (your dog backs off), or cross the street to avoid it.
  • 💦 Sprinklers go off at least twice and scare your dog. Treats and spray do not work. Cross the street!
  • 💛 Pee break: your dog stops for a moment on its own.
  • 💩 Poop: you must pick it up with a bag before you can move on.
  • Stay on the opposite side of the street from a problem and you skip it completely.

Supplies

8 treats, 2 poop bags and 5 sprays. Spend them wisely!

Controls

  • ↑ ↓ cross the street · 1 treat · 2 spray · 3 bag · Space hurry
  • On a phone, tap the buttons under the game. Hold Hurry to go faster.

The finish

Make it home and your dog gives you a big hug: "I love you. Thank you for my walk."
